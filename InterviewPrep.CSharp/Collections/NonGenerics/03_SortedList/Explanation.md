          ↓
Non-generic
          ↓
Key → Value
          ↓
Sorted by key
          ↓
Array-based
          ↓
Key lookup O(log n)
          ↓
Index access O(1)
          ↓
Insert/Remove O(n)
          ↓
Legacy
          ↓
