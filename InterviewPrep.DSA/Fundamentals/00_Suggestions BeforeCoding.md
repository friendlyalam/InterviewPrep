1. Trust the Process

There will be days when you feel:

"This topic is too easy."
"Why are we spending so much time on basics?"
"Can we jump to Dynamic Programming?"


=========================================================================================================
2. Learn to Think, Not Memorize

When I ask:

Which data structure would you choose?

Don't try to remember the "correct" answer.

Instead, think:

What operations are needed?
How often do we search?
How often do we insert?
How much memory can we use?

That's how engineers think.

===================================================================================================================
3. Master One Concept Before the Next

We'll never say:

"I've completed Arrays."

We'll say:

"I truly understand Arrays."

There's a big difference.

===============================================================================================================
4. Your Only Job

Your responsibilities are simple:

Attend our daily lesson.
Complete the homework.
Ask questions whenever you're unsure.
Revise on Sundays.
Stay consistent.

==========================================================================================================
5. Coding Rule

Whenever we solve a problem, follow this order:

Understand the problem.
Think of a brute-force solution.
Analyze it.
Improve it.
Write the code.
Dry run it.
Discuss complexity.

This is exactly how many interviewers expect candidates to approach problems.

==========================================================================================================

6. Don't Compare Yourself

Some people solve 500 LeetCode problems.

Others solve 150 but understand them deeply.

I'd rather you be in the second group.

Interviewers notice depth of understanding.

==========================================================================================================
